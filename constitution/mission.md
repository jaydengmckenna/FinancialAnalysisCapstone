# Project Mission

## Project Name

Agentic Financial Analysis Automation

## Mission Statement

The mission of Agentic Financial Analysis Automation is to help users
conduct preliminary investment research by transforming their investment
preferences into a structured, evidence-based financial research report.

The system uses AI agents and software tools to research potential
investment opportunities, compare relevant information, identify risks,
and present its findings for human review.

The system supports human decision-making but does not make investment
decisions or execute financial transactions. The final decision always
belongs to the user.

## Problem Statement

Investment research often requires users to collect information from
multiple sources, compare financial data, evaluate risks, and determine
whether potential investments align with their preferences.

This process can be time-consuming and inconsistent. Important information
may be overlooked, sources may conflict, and opinions may be mistaken for
verified facts.

This project addresses that problem by providing a structured research
workflow that gathers public information, organizes supporting evidence,
compares potential investments, and produces a transparent research report.

## Intended Audience

The system is intended for:

- Individual investors conducting preliminary research.
- Financial researchers comparing potential investments.
- Analysts organizing publicly available financial information.
- Learners exploring evidence-based investment research.

The system is not intended to replace a licensed financial professional or
serve as the sole basis for an investment decision.

## Primary Use Case

A user submits an investment research question together with relevant
preferences.

Example:

> What foreign stocks may be worth researching for someone with a high
> risk tolerance, a long-term investment horizon, and a growth objective?

The system researches potentially relevant investment candidates, compares
them using a documented methodology, identifies important risks, and
produces a report explaining which candidates appear most aligned with the
user's criteria.

The report is informational and is intended to support additional human
research and decision-making.

## Core Capabilities

The system is intended to:

- Accept an investment research question and user preferences.
- Create a structured research plan.
- Search the web for relevant public information.
- Retrieve information from selected public webpages.
- Organize financial evidence and source information.
- Compare potential investment candidates.
- Perform repeatable financial calculations.
- Identify risks, limitations, and missing information.
- Validate important claims against collected evidence.
- Generate a cited financial research report.
- Save recommendation details to a file.

## Guiding Principles

### Human Authority

The system supports human decisions but does not make decisions on the
user's behalf.

### Evidence-Based Research

Material conclusions should be supported by collected evidence or
repeatable calculations.

### Transparency

The report should explain how candidates were compared and distinguish
sourced facts from system-generated interpretation.

### Risk Awareness

Recommendations should include relevant risks, limitations, and
uncertainties rather than presenting outcomes as guaranteed.

### Source Traceability

Users and evaluators should be able to identify the sources supporting the
report's significant factual claims.

### Responsible Uncertainty

The system should disclose missing or conflicting information rather than
fabricating facts or overstating confidence.

## Project Boundaries

The system may research, compare, rank, and explain potential investments
for additional human consideration.

The system does not:

- Execute trades.
- Access brokerage accounts.
- Control or transfer funds.
- Manage or rebalance portfolios.
- Guarantee investment performance.
- Provide tax or legal advice.
- Conceal missing or conflicting evidence.
- Make the final investment decision for the user.

## Non-Goals

The initial version is not intended to provide:

- Autonomous portfolio management.
- Real-time market monitoring.
- High-frequency trading.
- Brokerage integration.
- Guaranteed market forecasts.
- A production financial-data platform.
- A production web or mobile application.
- Support for every financial instrument.

These capabilities are outside the scope of the capstone unless the project
specifications are intentionally revised.

## Definition of Success

The project fulfills its mission when it can:

1. Accept a financial research request and user preferences.
2. Search for and retrieve relevant public information.
3. Preserve supporting evidence and source details.
4. Compare potential investments using a documented approach.
5. Identify important risks and limitations.
6. Produce a readable, cited financial research report.
7. Save the recommendation details to a file.
8. Demonstrate that the output remains subject to human review.
9. Pass happy-path and adversarial tests.
10. Meet the required capstone documentation and architecture requirements.
