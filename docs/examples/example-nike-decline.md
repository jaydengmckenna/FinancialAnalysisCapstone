# Fiscal-Year Net Income Growth Analysis for Nike (FY2025 to FY2026)

## Research Question
What is Nike's fiscal-year net income growth from FY2025 to FY2026 in USD billions, and what qualified research conclusion can be drawn for a high-risk, long-term growth investor?

## Investor Profile
The investor profile is characterized by a high risk tolerance and a long-term investment horizon with an objective focused on growth. No exclusions or geographic or industry preferences were specified.

## Research Scope
The research scope is limited to public stock investment in Nike and considers fiscal-year net income growth as the key financial metric for evaluation.

## Executive Summary
This report analyzes Nike's net income growth rate from fiscal year 2025 to fiscal year 2026. Data from primary and secondary sources indicate a moderate decline in net income, with a calculated growth rate of -3.45%. Despite this decline, the workflow results in a qualified recommendation for Nike, supported by moderate confidence in the evidence and evaluations.

## Research Methodology
The research used evidence from two sources reporting Nike's net income in USD billions for FY2025 and FY2026. The net income values of 3.219 billion for FY2025 and 3.108 billion for FY2026 were used to calculate the net income growth rate via the formula ((current - previous) / previous) * 100, resulting in a -3.45% growth rate.
